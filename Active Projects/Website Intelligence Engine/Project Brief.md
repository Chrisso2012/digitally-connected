# Section 1 – Founder Brief

## Business Problem

Small businesses often have outdated, underperforming or poorly designed websites, but they don't realise the extent of the missed opportunities. Traditional website audits are time-consuming and manually produced, making them difficult to scale as a lead generation tool. Digitally Connected needs an automated system that can analyse a prospect's website, identify improvement opportunities, generate a branded audit report and optionally create a modern website preview using AI, all with minimal manual intervention.

---

## Desired Outcome

Create an automated Website Intelligence Engine that accepts a business website URL, analyses the site using AI, researches competitors, generates a professional branded audit report, produces a modern website preview, and delivers both to the prospect automatically within minutes. The system should become a repeatable lead generation asset and demonstrate Digitally Connected's AI and automation capabilities.

---

## Why Now?

Digitally Connected is establishing itself as an AI-first growth consultancy. Demonstrating practical AI solutions is essential for credibility and client acquisition. An automated Website Intelligence Engine provides immediate value to prospects, differentiates the business from traditional agencies, and creates a scalable sales asset.

---

## Constraints

Where practical, use existing tools already adopted by Digitally Connected, including n8n, GitHub, Claude, ChatGPT, Lovable and Google Workspace. The solution should minimise ongoing operating costs, maximise automation and remain modular so components can be replaced as better technologies emerge.
